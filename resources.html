Quick glossary

Premium What you pay monthly for coverage.
Deductible What you pay before coverage kicks in.
Copay A fixed fee per visit or prescription.
Coinsurance Your share of costs after the deductible.
Out-of-pocket max The most you’ll pay in a year.
Formulary The list of drugs a plan covers.
