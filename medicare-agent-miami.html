OUR STORY

Built for the community we know.

The Health Experts Insurance was built on a simple belief: every person deserves a Medicare broker who understands them — their language, their doctors, their community.

When Yahoska and Katy founded THEI in 2019, they saw a gap in South Florida's Medicare market: a largely Spanish-speaking senior community served by brokers who didn't speak their language, didn't know their neighborhoods, and disappeared after enrollment.

They built THEI to be different — independent, so they could always show every option from every carrier; bilingual, not as translation but as brokers who grew up in this culture; and local, with an office in Doral at the heart of the community they serve.

Today, THEI serves hundreds of clients across Miami-Dade and South Florida — helping Medicare-eligible seniors compare plans, navigate cost-saving programs like MSP and Extra Help, and make confident decisions about their healthcare.
