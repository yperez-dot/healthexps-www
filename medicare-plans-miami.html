Understanding Medicare

What is Medicare?

Medicare is federal health insurance for people 65+ (and some younger people with disabilities). It is made up of Part A (hospital), Part B (medical), Part C (Medicare Advantage — a private all-in-one alternative), and Part D (prescription drugs). Most people combine these into either a Medicare Advantage plan or Original Medicare plus a Supplement plus Part D. Our licensed brokers explain your options in plain language — in English or Español — at no cost to you.

Three ways to get Medicare coverage

Medicare Advantage
All-in-one plans offered by private insurers, often with $0 premiums and extra benefits like dental, vision, and drug coverage.
  • Many $0 premium plans in Miami-Dade
  • Annual out-of-pocket maximum protects you
  • Dental, vision & hearing often included
  • OTC allowance, gym & transportation extras
  • Network-based — we verify your doctors first
Best for: lower monthly costs + extra benefits
Learn about Medicare Advantage →
Medicare Supplement
Medigap coverage that works alongside Original Medicare — see any doctor nationwide who accepts Medicare, with minimal billing surprises.
  • No provider networks — total flexibility
  • Works with any doctor who accepts Medicare
  • Covers most out-of-pocket gaps
  • Predictable costs year over year
  • Ideal for frequent travelers
Best for: travel & maximum flexibility
Learn about Medicare Supplement →
Part D — Prescriptions
Standalone prescription drug plans. We check your exact medications against every formulary to find the plan that covers your drugs at the lowest cost.
  • Full formulary review, plan by plan
  • Compare copays for your specific drugs
  • Available as standalone or bundled with Advantage
  • Income-based extra help (LIS) available
  • Annual enrollment Oct 15 – Dec 7
Best for: managing prescription costs

COMMON MISTAKES

Medicare myths that cost Floridians thousands

One wrong assumption can mean permanent penalties or years of overpaying. Know the facts before you enroll.

MYTH
"I have to be retired to get Medicare."
FALSE — you qualify at 65 regardless of whether you are still working.
MYTH
"COBRA extends my Medicare window."
FALSE — your Special Enrollment Period clock starts when employer coverage ends, not COBRA.
MYTH
"A $0 plan is always the best deal."
FALSE — out-of-pocket costs can reach $9,250/year. Total cost matters more than premium.
