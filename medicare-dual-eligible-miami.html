Dual Eligible Plans — Florida 2026

Have Medicare & Medicaid? You may qualify for a $0-premium plan.

A D-SNP is a special type of Medicare Advantage plan built only for people who have both Medicare and Medicaid. It coordinates both programs into one card, keeps all your Medicaid benefits, and adds extra benefits at little or no cost to you.

What a D-SNP adds on top of Medicare & Medicaid

Benefits Original Medicare and Medicaid alone don’t cover — often at $0 premium.

Grocery & OTC allowance
A monthly spending card for groceries, over-the-counter items, and sometimes utilities or rent for those who qualify.
Free transportation
Rides to and from medical appointments and the pharmacy, included on most D-SNPs in Florida.
Dental, vision & hearing
Coverage Original Medicare doesn’t include — cleanings, glasses, and hearing aids.
Prescription drugs (Part D)
Built in, with very low copays thanks to Extra Help — as little as $0–$12.65 per drug in 2026.
$0 doctor & hospital costs
With QMB-level Medicaid, you’re protected from Medicare deductibles, copays, and coinsurance.
Care coordination
One plan, one card, one team helping you manage both Medicare and Medicaid benefits seamlessly.

Do you qualify? Florida 2026 income guide

If you have Medicare and limited income, you may qualify for full Medicaid or a Medicare Savings Program (MSP) — either can make you eligible for a D-SNP. Asset limit: $9,950 single / $14,910 married. Certain income doesn’t count — the only way to know for sure is to apply, and we help you do it free.

Don’t have Medicaid? You may still qualify — Chronic Condition Plans (C-SNP)

If you have Medicare and a serious chronic condition, you may qualify for a Chronic Condition Special Needs Plan (C-SNP) — even without Medicaid. C-SNPs often include the same extra benefits: grocery cards, OTC allowances, free transportation, and care teams built around your condition.

Monthly food & grocery card
$25–$150+/month for approved healthy foods at participating stores — including fresh produce, proteins, and diabetic staples.
OTC allowance
A monthly card for vitamins, diabetic supplies, wound care, pain relief, and more — used in-store or shipped to your door.
Condition-specific care team
One coordinated team managing your primary care, specialists, and medications around your specific diagnosis — fewer gaps.
Free transportation
Rides to medical appointments, labs, and the pharmacy included on most C-SNP plans in Florida.

Conditions that may qualify you for a C-SNP in Florida 2026

Diabetes (Type 1 or 2)
COPD / chronic lung disease
Heart failure / cardiovascular disease
Chronic kidney disease (CKD / ESRD)
Dementia / Alzheimer’s disease
HIV / AIDS
Cancer (certain diagnoses)
Autoimmune disorders

You don’t need Medicaid to qualify for a C-SNP — just a confirmed diagnosis from your doctor. Many people with chronic conditions are leaving free grocery and OTC benefits on the table without knowing it. We compare every C-SNP and D-SNP in your zip code at no cost.

Frequently asked questions

Can I enroll in a D-SNP any time of year?
Often yes. People with both Medicare and Medicaid usually qualify for a Special Enrollment Period and can switch or join a D-SNP once per quarter, plus during the Annual Enrollment Period (Oct 15–Dec 7). We can check your window today.
Will I lose my Medicaid if I join a D-SNP?
No — a D-SNP works alongside your Medicaid, it doesn’t replace it. You keep both, and the plan coordinates them for you. You just need to renew your Medicaid each year to stay eligible — and we help you do that.
How much does a D-SNP cost?
Most D-SNPs in Florida have a $0 monthly premium for people who qualify for both Medicare and Medicaid — your Medicaid covers what Medicare doesn’t. Our service is always free — we’re paid by the insurance carriers, never by you.
What if I have Medicare but not Medicaid?
You may still qualify for a Chronic Condition Special Needs Plan (C-SNP) with a confirmed diagnosis, or for a Medicare Savings Program that can open the door to a D-SNP — we’ll check both. Even if you think your income is too high, certain income doesn’t count, so it’s worth checking.
Do you speak Spanish?
Yes — our entire team is bilingual. Every step, every document, and every phone call can be in English or Spanish, whichever you’re most comfortable with. Same agent throughout, no handoffs.
Is there any cost to use your service?
No. Our consultations, plan comparisons, and enrollment help are completely free. We’re paid a commission by the insurance carriers — the same amount regardless of which plan you choose — so we always recommend what’s best for you, not what pays us the most.
