Frequently asked questions

Is the Medicare plan finder really free? +
Yes. The tool is completely free, and there’s no cost to enroll through our licensed brokers. We’re compensated by carriers, never by you. No obligation, and we don’t sell or share your information.
Which Medicare plans can I compare? +
2026 Medicare Advantage (Part C), standalone Part D drug plans, and Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plans available in your Florida ZIP code — across 14 carriers including Humana, UnitedHealthcare, Aetna, and Wellcare.
Do I need to enter personal information? +
You’ll need your ZIP code and current coverage type to see accurate options. Adding prescriptions or doctors gets you personalized estimates. We never require your Social Security or Medicare number to compare plans.
What if I need help understanding the results? +
Call 1-800-380-6821 or email info@healthexps.com. Our licensed bilingual brokers walk you through your options in English or Spanish, at no charge.
When can I switch my Medicare plan? +
Most people switch during the Annual Enrollment Period (Oct 15 – Dec 7) for coverage starting Jan 1. You may also qualify for a Special Enrollment Period if you moved, lost coverage, became eligible for Extra Help, or your plan is leaving Florida — like the AvMed exit.
