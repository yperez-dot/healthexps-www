# How Much Does Health Insurance Cost in Miami in 2027?

**The price of a health plan in Miami depends on five things the law lets insurers use, plus your income. Here's what Florida's own 2027 numbers show, what changes your price, and who can get help paying for it.**

<div class="quick-answer"><span class="qa-label">Quick answer</span>
<p>For 2027, Florida's Office of Insurance Regulation (OIR) estimates the <strong>average Silver plan for a 28-year-old at $776 a month in Miami-Dade and $723 in Broward</strong> before any subsidy. With the average federal subsidy for someone earning $36,000, that drops to about <strong>$381 in Miami-Dade and $312 in Broward</strong>.</p>
<p>Your price will be different: age, ZIP code, tobacco use, plan category and household size all change it. Open Enrollment for 2027 runs <strong>November 1, 2026, to January 15, 2027</strong>, and you should enroll by <strong>December 15</strong> for coverage on January 1.</p></div>

*Sources: Florida OIR 2027 county examples for a [single adult](https://www.floir.gov/docs-sf/life-health-libraries/federal-health-insurance/2027-plan-year/indiv-aca-average-single-36k.pdf) and a [family of four](https://www.floir.gov/docs-sf/life-health-libraries/federal-health-insurance/2027-plan-year/indiv-aca-average-family-4-87k.pdf), published October 16, 2026. OIR says these are illustrative averages, subject to final federal review, and that your actual premium and subsidy are on HealthCare.gov.*

## What Florida's 2027 examples show for Miami-Dade and Broward

These are the state's own examples for an average Silver plan. Silver is the most common category, with about 47% of Florida Marketplace enrollment, according to OIR.

| Example (monthly) | County | Average Silver premium | Average federal subsidy | Cost after subsidy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Single adult, age 28, earning $36,000 | Miami-Dade | $776 | $395 | $381 |
| Single adult, age 28, earning $36,000 | Broward | $723 | $411 | $312 |
| Family of 4 (two adults age 40, two children age 10), earning $87,000 | Miami-Dade | $2,917 | $1,698 | $1,219 |
| Family of 4 (two adults age 40, two children age 10), earning $87,000 | Broward | $2,717 | $1,758 | $959 |

Statewide, OIR reports that the average 2027 individual-market premium is **$898 per person per month before subsidies**, up **10.4%** from $811 in 2026, weighted by enrollment ([OIR rate summary](https://floir.gov/docs-sf/life-health-libraries/federal-health-insurance/2027-plan-year/aca-2027-web-tables_deliverable-part-1.pdf)). That's an average across all plans and ages, not a quote.

**Cheapest isn't always lowest cost.** Bronze plans usually cost less each month but have higher deductibles. Gold plans cost more each month but less when you get care. Compare your total yearly cost, not just the premium.

## What changes your price

Under the ACA, insurers can use only five factors to set a Marketplace premium ([HealthCare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov/how-plans-set-your-premiums/)):

1. **Age.** Premiums can be up to 3 times higher for older adults than for younger ones.
2. **Location.** Your ZIP code and county matter. That's why Miami-Dade and Broward prices differ.
3. **Tobacco use.** Insurers can charge tobacco users up to 50% more.
4. **Who's covered.** Adding a spouse or children raises the premium.
5. **Plan category.** Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum split costs differently. On average, the plan pays about 60% of covered costs with Bronze, 70% with Silver, 80% with Gold and 90% with Platinum ([HealthCare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov/choose-a-plan/plans-categories/)).

Your health, medical history and sex **can't** be used to set an ACA premium, and every Marketplace plan covers pre-existing conditions from the first day.

### Network type matters too

The network decides which doctors and hospitals you can use. **HMO** and **EPO** plans generally cover only in-network care, except emergencies. **PPO** plans let you go out of network for a higher cost ([HealthCare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov/choose-a-plan/plan-types/)). In Florida's 2027 individual-market rate summary, every insurer lists its network as an HMO or EPO ([OIR](https://floir.gov/docs-sf/life-health-libraries/federal-health-insurance/2027-plan-year/aca-2027-web-tables_deliverable-part-1.pdf)), so checking that your doctors are in the network is one of the most important steps.

## Who gets help paying in 2027

The premium tax credit lowers your monthly premium when you buy through the Marketplace. For 2027 coverage, you generally qualify if your household income is **between 100% and 400% of the federal poverty level (FPL)** ([HealthCare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov/glossary/federal-poverty-level-fpl/)). Marketplace savings for 2027 use the 2026 poverty guidelines from [HHS](https://aspe.hhs.gov/topics/poverty-economic-mobility/poverty-guidelines):

| Household size | 100% FPL | 400% FPL |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $15,960 | $63,840 |
| 2 | $21,640 | $86,560 |
| 3 | $27,320 | $109,280 |
| 4 | $33,000 | $132,000 |

*400% amounts are 4 times the HHS guideline for the 48 contiguous states. HealthCare.gov will post its 2027 income tables on November 1.*

**How much you pay:** for 2027, the IRS sets the share of income you're expected to pay for the benchmark Silver plan at **2.15% to 10.22%**, depending on your income ([IRS Rev. Proc. 2026-26](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-26-26.pdf)). The subsidy covers the rest of the benchmark price, and you can use it on any metal level.

**Extra savings on Silver:** if your income is low enough to qualify, cost-sharing reductions lower your deductible and copays, but only on Silver plans ([HealthCare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov/choose-a-plan/plans-categories/)).

A few other rules apply:

- **Below 100% FPL:** you probably won't qualify for Marketplace savings, but you may qualify for Medicaid ([HealthCare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov/glossary/federal-poverty-level-fpl/)).
- **Job-based coverage:** an affordable offer from your job, or a spouse's family coverage, usually means no subsidy.
- **Immigration status:** Treasury and the IRS have proposed rules that, beginning in 2027, limit the credit to U.S. citizens, U.S. nationals and certain lawfully present immigrants ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/treasury-and-irs-move-to-protect-health-care-benefits-for-eligible-americans)). If you're not a citizen, ask us to check before you apply.

## Earn too much for a subsidy? Your options

Above 400% FPL, you don't get the premium tax credit in 2027, so you pay the full price. You still have choices:

- **Compare total yearly cost, not just the premium.** A Bronze plan has a lower premium, while Gold may cost less overall if you see doctors often.
- **Look at HSA-eligible plans.** All Bronze and Catastrophic plans work with a Health Savings Account, which lets you set aside pre-tax money for medical costs ([HealthCare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov/choose-a-plan/plans-categories/)).
- **Under 30?** You may be able to buy a lower-premium Catastrophic plan ([HealthCare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov/choose-a-plan/catastrophic-health-plans/)).
- **Check your income estimate.** Savings are based on your expected modified adjusted gross income for 2027. Some adjustments, such as conventional IRA contributions, lower adjusted gross income ([HealthCare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov/glossary/federal-poverty-level-fpl/)). Ask your tax professional what applies to you.
- **Buy directly from an insurer.** ACA plans are also sold outside the Marketplace, with the same consumer protections but no subsidy. OIR's 2027 summary shows some insurers selling only off-exchange ([OIR](https://floir.gov/docs-sf/life-health-libraries/federal-health-insurance/2027-plan-year/aca-2027-web-tables_deliverable-part-1.pdf)).
- **Know the trade-offs of non-ACA plans.** Short-term plans aren't subject to federal rules that ban pre-existing condition exclusions or require comprehensive coverage ([CMS](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/short-term-limited-duration-insurance-and-independent-noncoordinated-excepted-benefits-coverage-cms)). We explain the differences before you buy anything.

## Key 2027 Open Enrollment dates

From [HealthCare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov/quick-guide/dates-and-deadlines/):

- **November 1, 2026:** Open Enrollment starts.
- **December 15, 2026:** last day to enroll for coverage starting January 1, 2027.
- **January 15, 2027:** Open Enrollment ends. Plans picked December 16 to January 15 start February 1.

After January 15, you can enroll only with a Special Enrollment Period, for example after losing job-based coverage, moving or having a baby ([HealthCare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov/coverage-outside-open-enrollment/special-enrollment-period/)). Losing a job plan? Compare [COBRA alternatives in Miami](/cobra-alternatives-miami/) first.

Already on a Marketplace plan? If you don't act by December 15, you may be automatically re-enrolled, so update your income and compare plans so you get the right subsidy ([HealthCare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov/keep-or-change-plan/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much is health insurance in Miami per month in 2027?

It depends on your age, ZIP code, tobacco use, plan category and income. For 2027, Florida's Office of Insurance Regulation estimates the average Silver plan in Miami-Dade at $776 a month for a 28-year-old before subsidies, or about $381 after the average federal subsidy for someone earning $36,000. Your real price is on your quote or [HealthCare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov/see-plans/).

### Is health insurance cheaper in Broward than in Miami-Dade?

In the state's 2027 Silver-plan examples, yes. The average Silver plan for a 28-year-old is $723 a month in Broward and $776 in Miami-Dade before subsidies. Prices still vary by plan, so compare the exact plans for your ZIP code.

### Who qualifies for help paying 2027 premiums?

Generally, households with income between 100% and 400% of the federal poverty level who buy through the Marketplace. For 2027 coverage that's about $15,960 to $63,840 for one person, or $33,000 to $132,000 for a family of four, based on the [2026 HHS poverty guidelines](https://aspe.hhs.gov/topics/poverty-economic-mobility/poverty-guidelines). Other rules apply, such as not having an affordable job-based plan.

### What if I earn too much for a subsidy?

You can still buy any Marketplace plan at full price, or an ACA plan sold directly by an insurer. Compare Bronze and Silver plans on total yearly cost, check whether an HSA-eligible plan fits, and make sure your income estimate is accurate. A broker can compare these at no cost.

### When can I sign up for 2027 coverage?

Open Enrollment runs November 1, 2026, to January 15, 2027. Enroll by December 15 for coverage that starts January 1. Plans picked December 16 to January 15 start February 1 ([HealthCare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov/quick-guide/dates-and-deadlines/)).

### Can my health affect what I pay?

Not for ACA plans. Insurers can't use your health, medical history or sex to set Marketplace premiums ([HealthCare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov/how-plans-set-your-premiums/)). Non-ACA plans such as short-term coverage play by different rules.
