**Two confirmed Southwest Florida Medicare Advantage network changes could affect where thousands of Lee and Collier County residents receive care beginning January 1, 2027.**

If you use **Lee Health** and have a **UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage plan**, or use **Naples Comprehensive Health (NCH)** and have a **Wellcare or Cigna/HealthSpring Medicare Advantage plan**, review your coverage before the 2026 Annual Enrollment Period ends on **December 7**.

## Who is affected?

| Health system | Medicare Advantage carrier | Effective date | Confirmed change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lee Health | UnitedHealthcare | January 1, 2027 | Lee Health hospitals and physicians will be out-of-network for affected UHC Medicare Advantage plans. |
| Naples Comprehensive Health (NCH) | Wellcare | January 1, 2027 | NCH will no longer accept Wellcare Medicare Advantage plans. |
| Naples Comprehensive Health (NCH) | Cigna/HealthSpring | January 1, 2027 | NCH will no longer accept Cigna/HealthSpring Medicare Advantage plans. |

Lee Health says its hospital and physician contracts with UnitedHealthcare end December 31, 2026, and that all Lee Health locations are affected beginning January 1. NCH states that its doctors and elective hospital services will no longer schedule members of the affected Medicare Advantage plans beginning January 1, 2027.

**Medicare Supplement (Medigap) is different.** Lee Health says members with traditional Medicare and a UnitedHealthcare or AARP Medicare Supplement are not affected by its UHC network change. NCH says it accepts all Medicare Supplement plans because Medigap is not based on provider networks.

**What this means:** a plan that works at one system may not work at the other. For example, Lee Health lists Wellcare, but NCH says it will stop accepting Wellcare Medicare Advantage on January 1, 2027. UnitedHealthcare is the reverse. Both systems also say they may not be in-network for every product a carrier offers, so check your **exact 2027 plan** and your doctors.

## Which Medicare Advantage plans Lee Health still lists

Lee Health's [Health Insurance Coverage page](https://www.leehealth.org/billing/insurance-help/health-insurance-coverage) lists these Medicare Advantage (Part C) plans as contracted with all Lee Health hospitals: Aetna, Align Senior Care of FL (I-SNP), Florida Blue, Florida Community Care (D-SNP and I-SNP), Freedom Health, HealthSpring (formerly known as Cigna Medicare), Humana, Optimum Health, UnitedHealthcare (ending effective December 31, 2026) and WellCare.

- **Not every product:** Lee Health says it "may not be a network provider for all products offered by a health plan," and that the list can change at any time.
- **Florida Blue:** Lee Health says that since September 1, 2024, Lee Physician Group primary care providers don't participate with Florida Blue Medicare Advantage plans.
- **UnitedHealthcare Marketplace plans:** Lee Health says UnitedHealthcare currently doesn't include Lee Health in its Exchange/Marketplace network.
- **Already have a 2027 Lee Health appointment?** Lee Health asks you not to cancel it without first understanding your options. Lee Health's community information line is 239-320-1234, and updates are posted at [leehealth.org/unitedhealthcare](https://www.leehealth.org/unitedhealthcare).

## Does NCH accept UnitedHealthcare?

**NCH hasn't announced any UnitedHealthcare change for 2027.** NCH's [2027 Medicare Advantage update](https://nchmd.org/maupdate/) names only two Medicare Advantage carriers it will stop accepting on January 1, 2027: **Wellcare** and **Cigna/HealthSpring**. On NCH's [Contracted Insurance Plans page](https://nchmd.org/patients-and-visitors/contracted-insurance-plans/), the **NCH Medical Group** (NCH's physician group) contracted-payer list includes "United Healthcare" and "United Healthcare – Individual & Family Plan" with no out-of-network date. Wellcare and Healthspring Medicare Advantage are both marked "Out of Network 12/31/26". The Naples Community Hospital list on the same page also includes United Healthcare.

The 2027 UnitedHealthcare network change in Southwest Florida is **Lee Health's**, not NCH's. Lee Health says its hospitals and physicians will be out-of-network for affected UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage plans beginning January 1, 2027.

NCH's list doesn't say which UnitedHealthcare plans are included. NCH also notes that it may not be a network provider for every product a carrier offers, and that individual doctors may participate with different plans than the hospital. So confirm your **exact 2027 UnitedHealthcare plan** with the plan and your doctors before you enroll.

### Other NCH Medical Group network notes

From the NCH Medical Group contracted-payer list on [NCH's Contracted Insurance Plans page](https://nchmd.org/patients-and-visitors/contracted-insurance-plans/):

- **Humana Medicare Advantage:** listed as "PPO ONLY." Humana HMO isn't listed for NCH Medical Group. The hospital list shows Humana Medicare Advantage HMO and PPO.
- **Florida Blue Medicare Advantage:** listed as "Out of Network for Primary Care 1/1/25."
- **Cigna – Connect:** listed as "Out of Network 12/31/26." Cigna Connect is Cigna's individual and family plan network, not a Medicare plan, and Cigna says it won't offer individual and family medical plans for 2027 ([Cigna](https://www.cigna.com/individuals-families/shop-plans/health-insurance-plans)).

For the full NCH Cigna/Wellcare details, see [NCH drops Cigna and Wellcare Medicare Advantage in 2027](/blog/nch-drops-cigna-wellcare-medicare-advantage-2027/).

## What happens if you do nothing?

Your insurance plan is not automatically cancelled. The change is whether the health system is considered in-network.

- **HMO members:** scheduled, non-emergency out-of-network care is generally not covered unless the plan authorizes it or another exception applies.
- **PPO members:** out-of-network benefits may be available, but deductibles, copays or coinsurance can be higher.
- **Emergency care:** go to the nearest emergency department. Emergency protections still apply regardless of network status.
- **Ongoing treatment:** some patients may qualify for temporary continuity-of-care protections. Call the member-services number on your insurance card promptly and ask how to apply.

Do not cancel a 2027 appointment until you confirm your options with the health system and your plan.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Lee Health leaving every UnitedHealthcare plan?

Lee Health says its hospitals and physicians will be out-of-network for affected UnitedHealthcare employer, individual and Medicare Advantage plans beginning January 1, 2027. UnitedHealthcare/AARP Medicare Supplement policies are not affected by this network termination. Verify your exact plan directly.

### Is NCH dropping Wellcare and HealthSpring in 2027?

Yes. NCH states that it will no longer accept Wellcare Medicare Advantage or Cigna/HealthSpring Medicare Advantage plans beginning January 1, 2027.

### Does this change my coverage now?

No. Both health systems state that current care remains unaffected through December 31, 2026. The network changes begin January 1, 2027.

### Must I change Medicare plans?

No. This information does not mean everyone should change. Review your exact network, benefits, prescriptions and costs. A licensed agent can explain available options, but the final decision is yours.

### Where can I find Wellcare's 2027 provider directory?

Wellcare's 2027 plan materials list its provider and pharmacy directories at [go.wellcare.com/2027providerdirectories](https://go.wellcare.com/2027providerdirectories). Choose your state, plan name, county and plan year, and remember that NCH says it will no longer accept Wellcare Medicare Advantage plans beginning January 1, 2027.

### When can I change my Medicare Advantage plan for 2027?

During the Medicare Annual Enrollment Period, October 15–December 7, 2026. Changes made during AEP take effect January 1, 2027.
