# Your 2027 Medicare AEP Checklist: What to Review Before October 15

**Medicare's Annual Enrollment Period (AEP) opens October 15 and closes December 7, 2026. Any change you make takes effect January 1, 2027.**

Plans can change their costs, drug lists and networks every year. A little preparation now, before the busy season starts, makes it much easier to decide whether to keep your plan or switch. This checklist walks you through it step by step.

<div class="alert-box"><strong>AEP for 2027 coverage: October 15 – December 7, 2026</strong><span>Medicare says the plan must get your enrollment request by December 7. Coverage you choose starts January 1, 2027.</span></div>

## Key dates for the 2027 plan year

| Date | What happens |
|---|---|
| September 2026 | Medicare Advantage and drug plans send your Annual Notice of Change (ANOC). |
| October 1, 2026 | Good time to review your plan's notices and start comparing options. |
| October 15, 2026 | AEP begins. You can join, switch or drop a Medicare Advantage or drug plan. |
| December 7, 2026 | AEP ends. In most cases, the last day to change your coverage for 2027. |
| January 1, 2027 | Your 2027 coverage begins. |
| January 1 – March 31, 2027 | Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period, only if you're already in a Medicare Advantage Plan. |

## What you can change during AEP

According to Medicare, during Open Enrollment you can:

- Join, drop or switch to another Medicare Advantage Plan, with or without drug coverage.
- Switch from Original Medicare to a Medicare Advantage Plan, or from a Medicare Advantage Plan back to Original Medicare.
- Join, drop or switch a Medicare drug plan (Part D) if you're in Original Medicare.

If your plan will still be offered in 2027 and it still fits your needs, you generally don't have to do anything; your coverage continues. The point of the checklist is to make sure it really does still fit.

## Your 2027 AEP checklist

<div class="checklist-box"><strong>Gather these before your review</strong><ul><li>Your Medicare card and your current plan's member ID card</li><li>Your 2027 Annual Notice of Change (ANOC) letter</li><li>A list of every medication, with the dose and how often you take it</li><li>Your pharmacy, plus every doctor, specialist and hospital you want to keep</li></ul></div>

1. **Read your ANOC.** It shows what changes in your plan on January 1: premium, copays, drug coverage, network and extra benefits. If you didn't get one, contact your plan.
2. **Check every medication.** Confirm each drug is on the plan's 2027 drug list (formulary), its tier, and any new rules like prior authorization.
3. **Check your pharmacy.** Make sure it's still in the network, and whether it's a "preferred" pharmacy.
4. **Check your doctors and hospitals.** Ask your doctors' offices which 2027 plans they accept, and confirm in the plan's 2027 provider directory.
5. **Compare total yearly cost, not just the premium.** Look at the monthly premium, deductibles, copays and the yearly maximum you could pay.
6. **Review the extras you use.** Dental, vision, hearing and other benefits can change from year to year.
7. **Compare at least two or three options** on Medicare Plan Compare or with a licensed agent.
8. **Don't wait until December.** Appointments fill up fast, and the plan must receive your enrollment by December 7.

## What's new for Part D drug costs in 2027

CMS has announced that in 2027 the Part D annual out-of-pocket threshold is **$2,400**. Once your out-of-pocket costs for covered Part D drugs reach that amount, you pay nothing more for covered Part D drugs for the rest of the year. Your plan's monthly premium doesn't count toward it, and each plan's own deductible and copays can differ, so confirm the details for your exact plan in its 2027 documents.

## If you have Medicare and Medicaid, or Extra Help

You may have more chances to make changes during the year. Medicare says people with Medicaid or Extra Help can change their drug coverage once a calendar month, and people with full Medicaid may be able to join an integrated Dual Eligible Special Needs Plan (D-SNP) monthly, if one is available. ## Frequently asked questions

### When is Medicare AEP for 2027 coverage?

Medicare's Open Enrollment runs October 15 to December 7, 2026. Changes you make during this time start January 1, 2027.

### What happens if I do nothing during AEP?

If your plan is still offered in 2027, your coverage generally continues with whatever changes are listed in your Annual Notice of Change. That's why it's worth reading.

### Can I change plans after December 7?

In most cases, December 7 is the last day to change your coverage for the next year. If you're in a Medicare Advantage Plan, you can make a change during the Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period (January 1 to March 31). Some life events also qualify you for a Special Enrollment Period.
