**Baptist Health's accepted insurance list for its Medical Group primary care physicians in Miami-Dade, Broward and the Upper Keys, as of October 1, 2026, says Preferred Care Network will exclude Baptist Health Medical Group Physicians, LLC as of January 1, 2027.**

Preferred Care Network is listed as a UnitedHealthcare company, under the plan name United MedicareMax. If you have that Medicare Advantage plan and see a Baptist Health Medical Group primary care physician, review your 2027 coverage before the Annual Enrollment Period ends on **December 7**.

The list is posted from Baptist Health's [Accepted Insurance](https://baptisthealth.net/patient-resources/insurance-accepted) page (the "Baptist Health Medical Group South Primary Care" file). You can also open [the October 1, 2026 primary care list (PDF)](https://baptisthealth.net/patient-resources/-/media/D4C27B2F56314000B809464F41058E65.ashx). Baptist Health says the list may be updated at any time and does not guarantee coverage; it also invites you to confirm benefits with your plan or with the Baptist Health Managed Care Call Center at 786-662-7667.

## What if you stay in Preferred Care Network?

Preferred Care Network plans are Medicare Advantage HMOs. According to [Medicare.gov](https://www.medicare.gov/health-drug-plans/health-plans/your-health-plan-options/HMO), in an HMO you generally must get your care and services from doctors, other health care providers and hospitals in the plan's network, except for emergency care, out-of-area urgent care, and temporary out-of-area dialysis. If you get health care outside the plan's network, you may have to pay the full cost. In most HMOs you also need to choose a primary care doctor and get a referral to see a specialist.

Do not cancel a 2027 appointment until you confirm your options with the doctor's office and your plan.

## Your action window: October 15–December 7, 2026

Medicare [Annual Enrollment (Open Enrollment)](https://www.medicare.gov/health-drug-plans/open-enrollment) runs from October 15 to December 7, 2026. The plan must get your enrollment request by December 7, and changes take effect January 1, 2027.

If you are already in a Medicare Advantage plan and miss December 7, [Medicare.gov](https://www.medicare.gov/basics/get-started-with-medicare/get-more-coverage/joining-a-plan) says the Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period, January 1–March 31, 2027, lets you make one change to another Medicare Advantage plan, or return to Original Medicare and join a separate drug plan. Coverage starts the first of the month after the plan gets your request.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which Baptist doctors does this apply to?

Baptist Health's accepted insurance list for Baptist Health Medical Group Primary Care Physicians in Miami-Dade, Broward and the Upper Keys, as of October 1, 2026, says Preferred Care Network will exclude Baptist Health Medical Group Physicians, LLC as of January 1, 2027. Confirm your exact doctor and 2027 plan with the plan and in the plan's 2027 provider directory.

### Does this mean Baptist hospitals and specialists are leaving Preferred Care Network too?

We cannot say. The change on Baptist Health's list is for its primary care physicians group. Confirm specialists and hospitals in your plan's 2027 provider directory, and with the plan, before you enroll.

### Does this change my coverage now?

The date on Baptist Health's list is January 1, 2027. Your 2026 care is not described as changing today. Confirm current access with your doctor's office and your plan.

### Is Traditional Medicare still accepted?

Yes. The same Baptist Health primary care list, as of October 1, 2026, states that Traditional Medicare is accepted.

### What if I stay in a Preferred Care Network HMO and my primary care doctor is a Baptist Health Medical Group physician?

Preferred Care Network plans are Medicare Advantage HMOs. [Medicare.gov](https://www.medicare.gov/health-drug-plans/health-plans/your-health-plan-options/HMO) says that in an HMO you generally must get your care from doctors, other health care providers and hospitals in the plan's network, except for emergency care, out-of-area urgent care, and temporary out-of-area dialysis. If you get care outside the plan's network, you may have to pay the full cost. Confirm your exact 2027 plan rules and your doctors.

### When can I change my Medicare Advantage plan?

During the Medicare Annual Enrollment Period, October 15–December 7, 2026. The plan must get your enrollment request by December 7, and changes take effect January 1, 2027. If you are already in a Medicare Advantage plan, the Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period, January 1–March 31, 2027, lets you make one change to another Medicare Advantage plan or return to Original Medicare. Source: [Medicare.gov](https://www.medicare.gov/health-drug-plans/open-enrollment).
