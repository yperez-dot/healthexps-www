# UHealth Leaving UnitedHealthcare Medicare? Your 2027 Miami Options

**UHealth (University of Miami Health System) will be out of network for UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage Preferred Care Network members beginning January 1, 2027.** Access was extended through December 31, 2026, so the 2026 Annual Enrollment Period is your window to plan.

If you have a **UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage plan** and see doctors through **UHealth**, including specialists at **Bascom Palmer Eye Institute** or **Sylvester Comprehensive Cancer Center**, review your coverage before the Annual Enrollment Period ends on **December 7**.

## Who is affected?

| Your UnitedHealthcare Medicare plan | UHealth network status | Key date |
|---|---|---|
| Preferred Care Network (Medicare Advantage) | In-network through December 31, 2026 (extended from the original September 1 end date) | Out of network beginning January 1, 2027 |
| Any other UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage plan | Out of network now | Already in effect |
| Emergency care at UHealth (any plan) | Always covered, regardless of network status | — |

According to UnitedHealthcare's update (July 16, 2026), UHealth is out of network for every UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage plan except Preferred Care Network, which had a separate agreement. UnitedHealthcare is ending that agreement too; the end date moved from September 1, 2026 to **January 1, 2027**.

**Don't mix this up with other UHealth contracts.** UHealth's contract for UnitedHealthcare Commercial, Exchange (Individual & Family) and Medicaid plans is a separate matter from the Medicare Advantage situation described here. UnitedHealthcare announced a multi-year renewal for those plans.

## Does my Medicare plan still cover Bascom Palmer or Sylvester?

If you're on a UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage plan that isn't Preferred Care Network, UHealth specialists, including Bascom Palmer Eye Institute and Sylvester Comprehensive Cancer Center, are already out of network. If you're on Preferred Care Network specifically, you have access through **December 31, 2026** (extended from the original September 1 end date).

If you're in active treatment for a serious condition (for example, ongoing chemotherapy or infusion therapy), you may qualify for **continuity of care**, a temporary extension of in-network benefits. UnitedHealthcare determines eligibility, not UHealth, so call the number on your plan ID card to ask directly.

## Health systems remaining in-network on January 1, 2027

For members on UnitedHealthcare Preferred Care Network plans, the following health systems will remain in-network in the South Florida region after UHealth exits:

- Baptist Health Medical Group Physicians
- HCA Florida Mercy Hospital
- Memorial Healthcare System
- Mount Sinai Medical Center of Florida

*Source: UnitedHealthcare member communication, August 2026.*

## Your action window: October 15–December 7, 2026

UHealth's exit takes effect **January 1, 2027**, the same day new Annual Enrollment Period (AEP) plans start. Any plan change must happen by **December 7**. Depending on your situation, your options generally fall into a few categories:

1. **Check which specific plan you have.** "UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage" covers several different plans in Miami-Dade and Broward, and only one of them (Preferred Care Network) still includes UHealth at all right now.
2. **Stay on your current plan** and find in-network providers, if that works for your care.
3. **Review other Medicare Advantage or Medicare Supplement plans** during this year's Annual Enrollment Period (Oct. 15 – Dec. 7, 2026).
4. **Talk to your doctor's office** about which insurance plans they're currently accepting, since that list is shifting as other carriers respond too.

None of these is automatically "right." It depends on which doctors matter most to you, what your current plan costs, and what else is available in Miami-Dade and Broward for 2027. That's the kind of comparison a licensed local agent can walk through with you at no cost.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is UHealth leaving every UnitedHealthcare Medicare plan?

UHealth is already out of network for every UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage plan except Preferred Care Network. Preferred Care Network members have access through December 31, 2026, and UHealth will be out of network for that plan beginning January 1, 2027.

### Does this change my coverage now?

Not for Preferred Care Network members: access continues through December 31, 2026. If you have a different UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage plan, UHealth is already out of network. Emergency care at UHealth is always covered.

### Can I keep seeing my UHealth doctor during treatment?

If you're in active treatment for a serious condition, you may qualify for continuity of care. UnitedHealthcare decides eligibility, so call the number on your plan ID card.

### Must I change Medicare plans?

No. This information does not mean everyone should change. Review your exact network, benefits, prescriptions and costs. A licensed agent can explain available options, but the final decision is yours.
