# Solis Health Plans Ending UHealth in 2027? What Miami Members Should Know

<p class="lang-switch-note"><a href="/es/blog/solis-uhealth-cambio-red-universidad-de-miami-2027/" hreflang="es" lang="es">Lea este artículo en español →</a></p>

**Solis Health Plans has decided to terminate the University of Miami Health System (UHealth) from its provider network, effective December 31, 2026.** If you're a Solis member and your doctors are at UHealth, the 2026 Annual Enrollment Period (October 15 – December 7) is your chance to plan for 2027.

UHealth includes specialists at **Bascom Palmer Eye Institute** and **Sylvester Comprehensive Cancer Center**. If you see any UHealth doctor today, take a few minutes to review your coverage before **December 7**.

## I'm in the middle of treatment at UHealth. What happens?

According to Solis Health Plans, members who are **actively receiving treatment** when UHealth's termination takes effect **may be eligible** to continue receiving covered services under Solis's **Continuity of Care policy** for **up to 60 calendar days** after the December 31, 2026 termination date.

Eligibility isn't automatic. Solis makes that decision, so call the member services number on your Solis ID card and ask whether your treatment qualifies, and for how long.

## What if I need a specialist after that?

Once the continuity-of-care period ends, Solis says members who need ongoing specialty care should **select a participating specialist within the Solis Health Plans provider network**. You can search for in-network doctors in the online Provider Directory at [solishealthplans.com](https://solishealthplans.com).

Before you pick someone new, it helps to ask the specialist's office whether they're accepting new patients and to confirm they're listed as in-network for your specific plan.

## Your action window: October 15–December 7, 2026

UHealth's termination from Solis takes effect **December 31, 2026**, right before new plans for 2027 start on January 1. The Annual Enrollment Period (AEP) runs **October 15 to December 7, 2026**, and it's when you can change your Medicare plan for 2027. Your options generally look like this:

1. **Make a list of the doctors who matter most to you**, and note which ones are part of UHealth.
2. **Stay with Solis** and move your care to in-network doctors, if that works for you. Use the Provider Directory at solishealthplans.com.
3. **Compare other Medicare Advantage or Medicare Supplement plans** during AEP if staying with your UHealth doctors is a priority for 2027.
4. **Talk to your doctor's office** about which Medicare plans they plan to accept in 2027.

None of these is automatically "right." It depends on your doctors, your prescriptions, what you pay today, and what's available where you live. A licensed local agent can walk through that comparison with you at no cost.

## Frequently asked questions

### When does UHealth leave the Solis Health Plans network?

Solis Health Plans set the effective termination date for the University of Miami Health System as December 31, 2026.

### Can I keep seeing my UHealth doctor during treatment?

If you're actively receiving treatment when the termination takes effect, you may be eligible to keep receiving covered services for up to 60 calendar days under Solis's Continuity of Care policy. Solis decides eligibility, so call the number on your Solis ID card.

### How do I find a new in-network specialist?

Use the online Provider Directory at solishealthplans.com to find participating specialists in the Solis network, or ask us for help with a free network check.

### Do I have to change Medicare plans?

No. This news doesn't mean everyone should switch. If your UHealth doctors are important to you, the Annual Enrollment Period (October 15 – December 7, 2026) is when you can review other plans for 2027. A licensed agent can explain your options, but the final decision is yours.
