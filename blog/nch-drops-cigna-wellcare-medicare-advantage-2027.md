# Naples hospital will drop two Medicare Advantage contracts in 2027

<div class="alert-box"><strong>Quick answer</strong><span><b>Who:</b> Wellcare Medicare Advantage and Cigna/HealthSpring Medicare Advantage members who use NCH hospitals or NCH Medical Group doctors. <b>When:</b> January 1, 2027. <b>Right now:</b> NCH says your NCH care is unaffected until then, so you can keep seeing your NCH providers through December 31, 2026.</span></div>

*Updated October 19, 2026: Medicare Annual Enrollment is now open. We added which plans NCH Medical Group still lists, what you can do during AEP and a free network check.*

Naples Comprehensive Health (NCH) says that **beginning January 1, 2027, it will no longer accept Cigna/HealthSpring Medicare Advantage plans and Wellcare Medicare Advantage plans** ([NCH 2027 Medicare Advantage update](https://nchmd.org/maupdate/)).

NCH says people who use NCH can keep in-network access by switching to a Medicare Advantage plan that's in-network with NCH, or to Original Medicare with a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) policy. Which of those fits you is a personal decision. A licensed agent can walk through it with you, but we don't pick the plan for you.

## Who is affected?

- **Wellcare Medicare Advantage members** who use NCH hospitals or NCH Medical Group doctors.
- **Cigna/HealthSpring Medicare Advantage members** who use NCH hospitals or NCH Medical Group doctors.
- **Cigna Connect members (not Medicare):** NCH's contracted-payer lists mark "Cigna – Connect" as "Out of Network 12/31/26." Cigna Connect is Cigna's individual and family plan network, and [Cigna says](https://www.cigna.com/individuals-families/shop-plans/health-insurance-plans) it won't offer individual and family medical plans for 2027.

What NCH says happens after January 1, 2027, if you stay in one of the affected Medicare Advantage plans:

- You'll likely have higher out-of-pocket costs or limited coverage, depending on your plan.
- NCH doctors and hospital elective services won't schedule appointments for members of these plans.
- NCH says it will always be available to address your emergency healthcare needs.

**Medicare Supplement (Medigap) is different.** NCH says it accepts all Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plans, and because these plans don't use provider networks, you don't have to worry about whether NCH is in or out of network.

## Which plans NCH Medical Group still lists

These Medicare-related entries appear on the **NCH Medical Group** (NCH's physician group) contracted-payer list on [NCH's Contracted Insurance Plans page](https://nchmd.org/patients-and-visitors/contracted-insurance-plans/), as of October 19, 2026:

- Aetna – Medicare
- Florida Blue – Medicare Advantage: "Out of Network for Primary Care 1/1/25"
- Florida Complete Care – Medicare Advantage
- Freedom Health – Medicare Advantage
- Humana – Medicare Advantage: "PPO ONLY" (the Naples Community Hospital list shows HMO and PPO)
- United Healthcare (the list doesn't say which UnitedHealthcare plans are included)
- UPMC Health Plan – Medicare Advantage

**Verify your exact plan.** NCH says that even when it has a contract with a health plan, it "may not be a network provider for ALL products offered by the carrier." It also says individual doctors may participate with different plans than the hospital. This list isn't a recommendation. Check the specific 2027 plan and your doctors before you enroll.

## What you can do during AEP (October 15–December 7, 2026)

According to [Medicare.gov](https://www.medicare.gov/basics/get-started-with-medicare/get-more-coverage/joining-a-plan), during Open Enrollment you can:

1. **Switch to another Medicare Advantage Plan**, with or without drug coverage. For example, you could choose a 2027 plan that's in-network with NCH.
2. **Switch from Medicare Advantage to Original Medicare**, and join a separate Medicare drug plan if you want drug coverage.
3. **Make sure the plan gets your request by December 7.** Your new coverage starts **January 1, 2027**.

**If you're thinking about Original Medicare plus Medigap:** Medicare.gov says that outside your one-time Medigap Open Enrollment Period, an insurance company can deny you a Medigap policy based on medical underwriting, or you may pay more ([Medicare.gov](https://www.medicare.gov/health-drug-plans/medigap/ready-to-buy)). Some people have "guaranteed issue rights," for example if they [drop a Medicare Advantage plan within their trial period](https://www.medicare.gov/health-drug-plans/medigap/basics/how-medigap-works). Check before you leave your Medicare Advantage plan. Medicare.gov also suggests checking with your State Insurance Department, because state law may give you extra rights.

## If you miss December 7

- **Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period (January 1–March 31):** if you're already in a Medicare Advantage Plan, [Medicare.gov](https://www.medicare.gov/basics/get-started-with-medicare/get-more-coverage/joining-a-plan) says you can switch to another Medicare Advantage Plan, or drop your plan and return to Original Medicare and join a separate drug plan. Coverage starts the first of the month after the plan gets your request.
- **Special Enrollment Periods:** Medicare.gov says these apply only in certain situations, like moving, losing or changing your current coverage, getting Medicaid or getting Extra Help. Don't assume a hospital network change qualifies. Call 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) to ask about your situation.

More options: [Missed Medicare Open Enrollment? What you can still do](/blog/missed-medicare-open-enrollment/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is NCH dropping Cigna and Wellcare Medicare Advantage in 2027?

Yes. NCH says that beginning January 1, 2027, it will no longer accept Cigna/HealthSpring Medicare Advantage plans or Wellcare Medicare Advantage plans.

### Does this change my NCH care in 2026?

No. NCH says these changes won't affect your benefits or care at NCH until January 1, 2027, and you can keep seeing your NCH providers as usual until then.

### Can I still use the NCH emergency room?

Yes. NCH says it will always be available to address your emergency healthcare needs. Its doctors and hospital elective services won't schedule appointments for members of the affected plans beginning January 1, 2027.

### Does NCH accept UnitedHealthcare?

NCH hasn't announced any UnitedHealthcare change for 2027. The NCH Medical Group contracted-payer list on NCH's Contracted Insurance Plans page includes United Healthcare with no out-of-network date. The list doesn't say which UnitedHealthcare plans are included, so verify your exact 2027 plan. The 2027 UnitedHealthcare network change in Southwest Florida is Lee Health's, not NCH's.

### Does NCH still accept Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plans?

Yes. NCH says it accepts all Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plans, and because these plans don't use provider networks, you don't have to worry about whether NCH is in or out of network.

### What if I miss December 7?

If you're in a Medicare Advantage Plan, Medicare says you can switch to another Medicare Advantage Plan or go back to Original Medicare during the Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period, January 1–March 31. Your change starts the first of the month after the plan gets your request.

### Where can I find Wellcare's 2027 provider directory?

Wellcare's 2027 plan materials list its provider and pharmacy directories at [go.wellcare.com/2027providerdirectories](https://go.wellcare.com/2027providerdirectories). Choose your state, plan name, county and plan year.
