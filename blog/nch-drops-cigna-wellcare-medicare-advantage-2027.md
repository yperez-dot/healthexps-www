# Naples hospital will drop two Medicare Advantage contracts in 2027

Naples Comprehensive Health told patients on August 26, 2026 that starting January 1, 2027, it will no longer accept Cigna Medicare Advantage plans or Wellcare Medicare Advantage plans.

NCH said people who use that hospital can still get care through Original Medicare, a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) policy, or a Medicare Advantage plan that stays in-network with NCH. ## Why Annual Enrollment Period exists

This is exactly why [Annual Enrollment Period](/medicare-annual-enrollment-2027/) exists. AEP runs October 15 through December 7, 2026 — and this year it matters more than most. Even if nothing else about your plan changed, a network change like this one can mean your hospital or doctor is suddenly out of network on January 1. AEP is open now, and the only way to know is to check your exact 2027 plan before December 7.

This is a local network change, not a new CMS enrollment rule — but it's a reminder that plans change every year, whether or not you hear about it directly. AEP is your window to review, compare, and switch if you need to.

## If you or someone you help uses NCH

Check whether your 2027 plan still includes that hospital and your doctors. Confirm it in the 2027 provider directory when it's posted — not last year's list. NCH is posting 2027 in-network Medicare Advantage information at [nchmd.org/MAUpdate](https://nchmd.org/MAUpdate).

## Looking for the Wellcare 2027 provider directory?

Wellcare's 2027 plan materials point members to its official provider and pharmacy directories at [go.wellcare.com/2027providerdirectories](https://go.wellcare.com/2027providerdirectories). Select your state, plan name, county, language and plan year. If your 2027 directory isn't available yet, call the member-services number on your Wellcare card.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is NCH dropping Cigna and Wellcare Medicare Advantage in 2027?

Yes. NCH says that beginning January 1, 2027, it will no longer accept Cigna/HealthSpring Medicare Advantage plans or Wellcare Medicare Advantage plans. Your NCH care is not affected until then.

### Does NCH still accept Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plans?

Yes. NCH says it accepts all Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plans, and because these plans don't use provider networks, NCH network status is not an issue for them.

### Where can I find Wellcare's 2027 provider directory?

Wellcare's 2027 plan materials list its provider and pharmacy directories at [go.wellcare.com/2027providerdirectories](https://go.wellcare.com/2027providerdirectories). Choose your state, plan name, county and plan year.

### When can I change my Medicare Advantage plan for 2027?

During the Medicare Annual Enrollment Period, October 15–December 7, 2026. Changes made during AEP take effect January 1, 2027.
