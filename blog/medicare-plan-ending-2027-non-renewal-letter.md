# Your Medicare Plan Is Ending in 2027: What a Non-Renewal Letter Means

**A Plan Non-Renewal Notice means your Medicare Advantage or drug plan is leaving the Medicare program next year. You'll need to choose new coverage for 2027.**

Getting this letter can be stressful, but you have options and time to act. You don't lose Medicare itself, and you have more than one enrollment window. Here's what the letter means and what to do next.

| When | What it means for you |

| October 15 – December 7, 2026 | Annual Enrollment Period. The smoothest time to choose a new plan, so your new coverage starts January 1, 2027. |
| December 8, 2026 – February 28, 2027 | Special Enrollment Period for plans whose Medicare contract isn't renewed. You can still switch to another plan. |

| Up to 60 days before, and no more than 63 days after, your plan coverage ends | Window to apply for a Medigap policy with guaranteed-issue rights, if you go to Original Medicare. |

## What a non-renewal letter is, and what it isn't

- **It is:** notice that your plan is leaving the Medicare program in the coming year. Medicare says you must look for a new plan for coverage next year.
- **It isn't:** a change to your Medicare Part A or Part B. You keep Original Medicare no matter what.
- **It's different from** a doctor leaving your network or a drug changing tiers. Those changes show up in your Annual Notice of Change (ANOC) and don't mean your plan is ending.

## Your two main paths

1. **Choose another Medicare Advantage Plan.** Pick one during AEP (by December 7) so it starts January 1, or use your Special Enrollment Period through February 28, 2027.
2. **Go to Original Medicare.** You can add a separate Medicare drug plan (Part D) for prescriptions, and you may be able to buy a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) policy with guaranteed-issue rights.

If you do nothing and don't join another Medicare Advantage Plan, Medicare says you'll be enrolled in Original Medicare when your plan ends. Original Medicare doesn't include most prescription drug coverage, so don't let a drug-coverage gap catch you by surprise.

## Medigap guaranteed-issue basics

When your Medicare Advantage Plan is leaving Medicare or will no longer cover your area, you have a **guaranteed-issue right** to buy certain Medigap policies. That means an insurance company can't turn you down for a covered policy because of your health.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I lose Medicare if my plan isn't renewed?

No. Your plan is leaving, not Medicare. If you don't join another Medicare Advantage Plan before your plan ends, Medicare says you'll be enrolled in Original Medicare.

### How long do I have to pick a new plan?

You can choose during Open Enrollment (October 15 to December 7, 2026). If your plan's Medicare contract isn't renewed, Medicare also gives you a Special Enrollment Period from December 8 through the last day of February of the following year.

### Can I buy a Medigap policy without health questions?

If your Medicare Advantage Plan is leaving Medicare, you have guaranteed-issue rights to buy certain Medigap policies. Medicare says to apply no more than 63 days after your coverage ends, and you can apply as early as 60 days before it ends.

### What should I do with the letter?

Keep it. It's your proof that your coverage is ending, and you may need it for your Medigap application.
