UNDERWRITING

When can you enroll without health questions?

Medigap insurers can ask about your health — and deny coverage or charge more — except during specific protected windows.

Medigap Open Enrollment Period (OEP)
A one-time 6-month window starting the month you're 65 and enrolled in Part B. Guaranteed acceptance — no health questions, no denials. This is the most important window.
Guaranteed Issue (GI) Rights
Certain situations give you GI rights outside of OEP — insurers must sell you a plan at standard rates. Common triggers:
  • Losing Medicare Advantage coverage (plan leaves area or goes bankrupt)
  • Leaving a Medicare Advantage plan within the first 12 months (trial right)
  • Moving out of your plan's service area
  • Losing employer or union group coverage
No Birthday Rule in Florida
Unlike some other states, Florida does not have a birthday rule. There is no annual window to switch Medigap plans without underwriting. To change plans, you must qualify medically — insurers can ask health questions and may charge more or deny coverage based on your history.
Outside protected windows
Insurers can ask health questions, charge higher premiums based on your health history, or deny coverage altogether. Don't miss your OEP — it's your best chance to lock in a plan.
ENROLLMENT PERIODS

When can you sign up for Medigap?

Medigap doesn't have a recurring annual enrollment period like Medicare Advantage. Timing matters a lot.

Initial Enrollment Window
Your 6-month Medigap OEP begins the first month you have both Part B and are 65 or older. This is your best — and often only — guaranteed window. Plans can't charge you more or deny you based on health.
Medicare Advantage Trial Right
If you join a Medicare Advantage plan for the first time and decide to switch back to Original Medicare within 12 months, you have a guaranteed right to buy Medigap Plan A, B, C, F, K, or L from any insurer in your state.
Annual Election Period (Oct 15 – Dec 7)
This period is for Medicare Advantage and Part D — not Medigap. You cannot use AEP to enroll in or change your Medigap plan. However, if you're leaving an Advantage plan during AEP, you may qualify for GI rights.
Florida Has No Birthday Rule
Florida does not offer an annual birthday window like some other states. Outside of your initial enrollment or federal guaranteed issue triggers, switching Medigap plans requires passing medical underwriting. Talk to a licensed broker to review your options.
All other times
You can apply for Medigap anytime — but outside protected windows, the insurer can underwrite your application. A licensed broker can evaluate your situation and tell you your best options.

Is Medigap right for you?

Medicare Supplement tends to be the better fit if any of these apply to your situation.

You use healthcare frequently
Ongoing conditions, regular specialist visits, or chronic illness make predictable costs more valuable than low premiums.
You travel inside the U.S.
Medigap lets you see any doctor that accepts Medicare anywhere in the country — no network, no service area restrictions.
Your doctors are not in MA networks
If your preferred doctors or hospital do not participate in Medicare Advantage networks, Medigap is often the only way to keep them.
You want zero billing surprises
With Plan G, once you meet your $283 Part B deductible, Medicare and Medigap cover the rest. No surprise bills for covered services.
