What is it?

What is final expense insurance?

Final expense insurance (also called burial or funeral insurance) is a small whole life policy designed to cover end-of-life costs. Unlike term life, it never expires and premiums never increase.

Medicare Gap Alert: Medicare doesn’t cover funeral costs — Social Security pays only $255 as a lump-sum death benefit. The average funeral in Florida costs $8,000–$12,000+. Without a plan, that burden falls entirely on your family.
  • Coverage $5,000–$50,000
  • Available ages 50–85
  • No medical exam required
  • Fixed premiums that never increase
  • Permanent coverage — never expires
  • Builds cash value over time
  • Tax-free death benefit
