High-deductible G

What changes from G
You pay eligible Medicare cost-sharing before the policy starts paying benefits. Medicare still pays its share.

$3,050. This is not a cap on all health-care spending, and it is not an out-of-pocket maximum.
Part B deductible

3. Decide what matters to you

  • More predictable cost-sharing? Compare the added Plan G premium with the costs you would otherwise pay yourself.
  • Comfortable with visit copays? Weigh Plan N’s premium difference, office and ER use, and potential excess charges.
  • Comfortable with a larger deductible? Weigh high-deductible G’s premium difference against your ability to pay eligible cost-sharing toward $3,050.
  • Before choosing: Use real quotes, confirm eligibility and underwriting, and review policy benefits. No plan is selected for you here.
Assumptions and what this does not include

Not included in this comparison

Part B and Part D premiums, any Part A premium, prescription drug costs, and services Medicare or the policy does not cover.

No all-in health-care total

We do not forecast visits, hospital stays, rate increases, inflation, or future claims. This page does not produce a 2027 total. Premiums and benefits can change.

2027 benefit reference

High-deductible G: $3,050 annual deductible, effective January 1, 2027 (CMS). The 2027 Part B deductible is not yet announced as of November 7, 2026. No 2026 Part B amount is substituted into a 2027 total.

Deductible is not a spending ceiling

The high deductible applies to eligible Medicare cost-sharing. Premiums, uncovered services, and other costs can remain. Policy limits and terms apply.

How inputs work

Each new entry replaces that plan’s sample value. Unedited plans stay marked as samples. Blank, zero, negative, and invalid inputs are excluded — not treated as free. Changes last only while this page is open. Florida has no Medigap birthday rule.
