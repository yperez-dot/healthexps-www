Coverage, explained simply

No jargon — just what each type actually means for you.

Term Life
Coverage for a set number of years (10, 20, 30) at the lowest cost. Best if you want maximum protection while raising kids or paying off a mortgage.
Whole Life
Coverage for your entire life with a cash value that grows over time. Costs more, but it never expires and can build savings.
Universal Life
Permanent coverage with flexible premiums and a cash value component. More adjustable than whole life, but more complex.
Rule of thumb, if you want a shortcut
A common starting point is 10x your annual income, plus enough to pay off your mortgage and other debts. The calculator above gives you a more personalized number.
