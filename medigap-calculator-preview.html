How we calculated these numbers ▾
Real, verified figures
  • Part B premium: $202.90/month (CMS, 2026)
  • Part B deductible: $283/year (CMS, 2026)
  • High-Deductible Plan G total deductible: $2,875/year (Medicare.gov Plan Finder, 2026)
  • Plan G premium default ($374/mo): median across all Florida carriers on Medicare.gov, blended for men and women, age 65 non-smoker
  • Plan N premium default ($287/mo): same method, full carrier list
  • HD Plan G premium default ($127/mo): same method, full carrier list
  • Plan N and HD Plan G increase rates (both 2.4%/year): derived from real Medicare.gov premiums at multiple ages — Plan N at 65/70/75, HD Plan G at 65/75. The two landed within a rounding error of each other, which is a good consistency check. See note below on what this does and doesn't prove
  • Plan G's increase rate (2.1%/year): same method, using real premiums at 65 ($374), 70 ($405), and 75 ($458) — close to Plan N and HD Plan G's rate, another good consistency check
  • Plan N copay structure: up to $20/office visit, up to $50/ER visit (standardized nationally by CMS)
  • What each plan covers (the benefits table above): standardized nationally, same for every carrier
Still simplified
  • Sex-blended pricing: since we don't ask male/female, the default hides real price differences between the two — anyone who wants their actual rate should call or use "I know my exact quotes"
  • Only ages 65, 70, and 75 have real data — ages in between or beyond are interpolated using the rates above, not individually verified
Important nuance: comparing 65/70/75-year-olds today shows how much MORE an older applicant pays right now — a reasonable stand-in for how one person's own premium grows over time, but not the same thing as tracking one real policy's price over 10 years.
