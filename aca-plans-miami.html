What changed in 2026
The enhanced subsidies in place from 2021–2025 expired on January 1, 2026. Premium tax credits still exist, but the rules reverted: help is available between 100–400% of the federal poverty level, and above that line there's no subsidy at all. If your plan got more expensive this year, this is why — it's worth re-comparing your options.

What is the ACA Marketplace?

The ACA Marketplace (HealthCare.gov) is where individuals and families under 65 without employer coverage compare ACA-compliant plans across four metal tiers. Income-based subsidies can cut your premium significantly — sometimes to $0.

Who is the ACA Marketplace for?

Self-employed & freelancers
1099 income or contract work means no employer coverage — this is your path to individual coverage with possible subsidies.
Families without employer coverage
If your employer's plan is too costly or unavailable, the Marketplace may be more affordable — especially with tax credits.

Do you qualify for subsidies?

Most people who apply are surprised by how much financial help is available. Two types of savings apply to ACA plans:

Premium Tax Credits (APTC)
Reduces your monthly premium. Available to individuals earning between 100%–400% of the federal poverty level. In 2026, a single person earning up to ~$62,600 may qualify; a family of 4 up to ~$128,600. Many pay $0–$50/month.

Cost-Sharing Reductions (CSR)
If your income is between 100%–250% of the federal poverty level, you may also qualify for CSR — lowering your deductible, copays, and out-of-pocket maximum on Silver plans. When can you enroll?

ACA enrollment isn't open year-round — but qualifying life events open a Special Enrollment Period anytime.

Open Enrollment
Nov 1 – Jan 15. Coverage starts Jan 1 (enroll by Dec 15) or Feb 1 (enroll by Jan 15).
Lost job coverage
Losing employer insurance triggers a 60-day SEP — the most common qualifying event.
Marriage or divorce
Getting married, divorced, or legally separated qualifies you for an SEP.
Birth or adoption
Having or adopting a child opens an SEP and lets you add them to your plan.
