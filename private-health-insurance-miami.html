What is private health insurance?

Any plan you buy directly — rather than through an employer or Medicare — is "private" coverage. That includes ACA Marketplace plans (bought "on-exchange," with possible subsidies) and off-exchange plans sold directly by carriers. Both cover individuals and families; the right one depends on your income and timing.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions about private health insurance

What's the difference between private health insurance and an ACA Marketplace plan?+
ACA Marketplace plans are standardized by metal tier (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum) and may qualify for income-based subsidies. Private health insurance is purchased directly, isn't subsidized, and often gives you more flexibility in plan design and network — it tends to be the better fit once ACA subsidies stop making sense for your income.
Who should consider private health insurance instead of an ACA plan?+
Private insurance often makes sense if your household income is above the ACA subsidy cliff, if you want a broader network or specific plan features the Marketplace doesn't offer, or if you're shopping outside ACA Open Enrollment and don't qualify for a Special Enrollment Period.
Can I enroll in private health insurance any time of year?+
Yes — unlike the ACA Marketplace, private health insurance isn't limited to an annual enrollment window. You can apply and enroll whenever you need coverage.
How does private health insurance compare in cost to an ACA plan?+
It depends entirely on your income and household size — if you qualify for ACA subsidies, the Marketplace is usually cheaper. If you're above the subsidy cliff, a private plan can often cost less than an unsubsidized ACA plan for comparable coverage. Our brokers will run both side by side for your specific numbers.
Can I switch between a private plan and an ACA plan later?+
Yes, though timing depends on your situation — ACA enrollment is generally limited to Open Enrollment or a qualifying life event, so we'll walk you through what's possible and when.
Does private health insurance cover pre-existing conditions?+
It depends on your specific plan and situation. Before you enroll, one of our licensed brokers will walk you through exactly what's covered and what isn't — so there are no surprises later.
