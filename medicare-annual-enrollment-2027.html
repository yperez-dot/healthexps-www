KEY DATES

The 2026 Annual Enrollment Period — what to expect and when

Mark these dates. Missing the Dec 7 deadline means waiting another full year to make changes to your Medicare coverage.

Late September
You receive your ANOC

Your Annual Notice of Change arrives by mail. This document shows every change your current plan is making for 2027 — premiums, copays, drug tiers, and network changes. Read it carefully.

October 1 – 14
Shopping window opens — review your benefits

2027 plan details become available. This is the best time to compare your plan options against alternatives — before enrollment officially opens. Call us and we'll do the comparison for you.

October 15
Annual Enrollment Period opens

You can now officially enroll, switch, or drop a Medicare plan. Changes made during AEP take effect January 1, 2027. Don't wait until December — the best appointments fill up fast.

December 7 — Deadline
AEP closes — deadline to make changes

This is your last day to enroll or switch plans. Miss this and your current plan rolls over for another year — even if premiums went up or your doctor left the network.

January 1, 2027
New coverage takes effect

Any changes made during AEP go live. Your new plan, benefits, premiums, and network apply starting today.

WHY REVIEW EVERY YEAR

5 reasons your 2026 plan may not be right for 2027

 

1
Your premium may have gone up

Medicare Advantage plans adjust premiums, copays, and deductibles every year. What was $0/mo in 2026 may not be $0/mo in 2027.

2
Your doctor may no longer be in-network

Plans update their provider networks annually. Your primary care doctor or specialist may have dropped out — and you won't know until your next visit.

3
Your medications may cost more

Drug formularies change every year. A medication that was Tier 2 in 2026 could move to Tier 4 in 2027 — costing you significantly more per month.

4
A plan that better suits your needs may now be available

New plans enter the market every year. A plan with benefits that better match your health situation, doctors, and budget may now be available in your ZIP code.

5
Your health needs may have changed

A new diagnosis, a new specialist, or a new medication changes which plan is the best fit. What worked in 2026 may not be optimal in 2027.
