What is the Medicare Savings Program?

MSP is a set of Florida-run programs (administered by the Department of Children and Families) that help people with limited income pay for Medicare — premiums, deductibles, and sometimes copays. There are four programs: QMB, SLMB, QI, and QDWI.

If you qualify for QMB, SLMB, or QI, you automatically qualify for Extra Help — federal assistance that can save up to $5,300/year on Part D drug costs, no separate application needed. Qualify for MSP? You may also be eligible for a $0-premium Dual Eligible (D-SNP) plan →

The 4 Medicare Savings Programs

Each program has different income limits and covers different costs. QMB is the most comprehensive.

Don’t have 40 work credits? QMB can still help.
If you need to buy into Medicare Part A because you have fewer than 40 work credits, QMB is the only MSP program that covers your Part A premium. SLMB and QI only help with Part B.

Must be done by phone — not online. Call SSA at 1-800-772-1213 and ask to schedule a phone appointment specifically to apply for “Conditional Part A and Part B.” During the appointment, use these exact words: “I am applying for Conditional Part A because I have fewer than 40 work credits and I am applying for the QMB program through Florida Medicaid.”

If the agent seems unfamiliar with the process, ask them to look up POMS Section HI 00801.140 — the federal policy (updated Jan 2025) that instructs them to bill Florida Medicaid instead of you.

Step 1 — Call SSA at 1-800-772-1213 (cannot be done online). Ask to schedule a phone appointment to apply for “Conditional Part A and Part B.” During the appointment, say: “I am applying for Conditional Part A because I have fewer than 40 work credits and I am applying for the QMB program through Florida Medicaid.” This protects you from being billed — the government bills Florida instead while your QMB is processed.

If this happens, politely ask them to look up POMS Section HI 00801.140 — the official federal policy (updated Jan 2025) that instructs agents to bill Florida Medicaid instead of the applicant. Florida is a Part A Buy-in State, meaning the state is required to cover this premium for approved QMBs.

Step 2 — Apply for QMB through Florida DCF at access.florida.gov. Bring proof of income, recent bank statements (under $9,950), and your Florida ID.

Step 3 — Wait ~30 days. Once approved, Florida notifies the federal government and your Part A and Part B premiums are covered. Providers cannot charge you deductibles or copays for Medicare-covered services.
